FamPaw Care

Privacy Policy

Student project – version 1.0

1. What we keep

Your name, email address, and password; the names, birthdays, and contact numbers of family members; pet details; and the health records, appointments, vaccinations, and reminders that you add.

2. Who can see it

Only the members of your family group can see your records, and only as far as their permissions allow. A family group cannot see another family's records.

3. How it is protected

Users must log in before they see private records. When the full system is online, passwords will be stored in a protected (hashed) form, never as plain text, and the site will use HTTPS.

4. This demo version

In this version of the project, everything you type is saved only in your own web browser on this device. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing your browser data, or using the delete options in Settings, removes it.

5. Your choices

You can ask to correct or delete the information you entered. Please contact the project owner (the student developer) with your request.
