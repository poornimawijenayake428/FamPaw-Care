FamPaw Care

Terms of Service

Student project – version 1.0

1. What FamPaw Care is

FamPaw Care is a family and pet health management system. It helps you keep health records, appointments, vaccination dates, and reminders in one place. It was created as a university project.

2. Not medical advice

FamPaw Care only stores information that you enter. It does not diagnose illnesses, prescribe medicine, or give emergency help. It does not replace a doctor or a veterinarian. Please ask a qualified professional before making any medical decision or changing any dose.

3. Your responsibilities

Please enter correct and up-to-date information. Reminders depend on the dates you enter. Keep your password private, and only share family information with people you trust.

4. Family groups

Only the members of your family group can see your family and pet records. Only people with the right permission can add, change, or delete shared records.

5. Changes

This is a project version. The rules on this page may change as the system is developed.
